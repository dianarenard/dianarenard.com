# dianarenard.com design

Diana's personal site: about her first, her products second. Read this before changing any page.

## The look (approved by Diana, 26 Oct 2026)

- **Colours:** wine `#150D10` for the hero, the Work section and the footer; crimson `#C0122C` for buttons and the
  accent words; rose `#FF5A73` for accent words on wine; lilac `#C9A7F5` for small labels on wine; cream `#FFFBF9`
  page background; blush `#F8EEF1` for the follow band.
- **Fonts:** Fraunces for headlines, labels and quotes (one or two words of a headline in italic crimson or rose);
  Figtree for body text and buttons.
- **Photos:** real photos of Diana, rounded corners. Hero: the portrait in the white blazer. Profile card: the red
  dress. On stage: the AI Salon Miami podium, the microphone, the evening event, her Instagram video. Only Diana
  in the frame; crop out other people's faces.
- **Products:** shown in the Work section, each card in its own brand (Regime, Mindologue, the book's black and white
  cover, NAPKIN with the real napkin).
- **Words:** praise her, generously and truthfully. No long dashes. Instagram is diana_renard_official, LinkedIn is
  /in/dianarenard, email diana@mindologue.com.

## Never

- **Coloured lines beside or above text:** no bar down the left of a quote or excerpt, no stripe on top of a card
  or a number, no rule trailing after a heading, no coloured top border on a box.
- **Small spaced-capital labels** (uppercase with wide letter-spacing). Labels are written normally, in Fraunces.
- **Fonts that read as AI-made:** no Inter, Roboto, Arial, Space Grotesk, Geist or Instrument Serif.
- Don't redesign the site away from this look. Change words and photos inside it.

## Publishing

GitHub Pages serves the `main` branch. Work on a branch, show Diana, and push to `main` only when she says "publish".
