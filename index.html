From an aerospace university to apps that read people.

Diana Renard studied engineering at Siberian State Aerospace University in Krasnoyarsk. She learned to see problems as systems: what goes in, what comes out, and the one wrong assumption that breaks everything after it.

Then her own life came apart. Broken relationships, work and money pressed in at once, and drawing became the way she understood her fears. She studied psychology and art therapy, and turned the exercises that carried her through into a 30-day workbook, Unfolding Your True Self.

The workbook became Mindologue, an app that reflects on what a drawing may be expressing, which she co-founded with Fabrice Colozzi. Then came a first date. She asked a charming man to draw on a napkin, read it with Mindologue, and chose him anyway. Everything the drawing showed came true. That napkin became NAPKIN.

Regime brings the same thinking to the body, with a plan that knows which week of her cycle a woman is in. Diana designs and builds Regime and NAPKIN herself.

"Most people struggle to put their deepest blocks into words, so I teach them to use symbols, lines, and colors instead."

Short bio

Diana Renard is an engineer, an author and the founder of three companies: Mindologue, an app that reflects on what your drawings express; Regime, health technology that starts from the female body; and NAPKIN Dating, the first-date app that reads a napkin drawing. Full bio

Diana Renard is the founder of Mindologue, Regime and NAPKIN Dating. She studied engineering at Siberian State Aerospace University in Krasnoyarsk, then psychology and art therapy, and is a certified art life coach. During a personal crisis, drawing became her way to understand her own emotions. That practice became her workbook, Unfolding Your True Self, and Mindologue, an app that helps people reflect on what their drawings may express. Regime applies the same thinking to the body: a health plan that adapts to a woman's hormones and life stage every day. NAPKIN Dating began on a first date, with a napkin drawing whose warning she ignored; the app invites a date to draw love on a napkin and shows who is sitting across the table. She designs and builds Regime and NAPKIN herself, and lives in Miami Beach.
